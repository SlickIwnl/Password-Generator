Most password generators just spit out random characters and call it a day — they don't tell you why something's actually strong. This one does both. Pick your length and character sets, and it generates a password using your browser's built-in crypto (not Math.random, actual cryptographically secure randomness), then breaks down the entropy and estimates crack time across a few real attack scenarios — from a slow throttled online guess to a GPU doing 10 billion guesses a second. Everything runs locally. Nothing gets logged or sent anywhere.
